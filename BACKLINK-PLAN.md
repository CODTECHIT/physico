# Flexo Physio: White-Hat Local Backlink Strategy

## 1. Local Directory Submissions (High Authority)
- **Justdial Hyderabad:** Essential for local lead generation.
- **Sulekha Hyderabad:** High-traffic local services directory.
- **Yellow Pages India:** Reliable local citation source.
- **IndiaMART:** For listing specialized medical equipment services.

## 2. Health & Wellness Blog Outreach
- Identify local Hyderabad bloggers (e.g., "Hyderabad Foodie," "Health & Fitness Hyderabad").
- Propose guest posts on "How IT professionals in Hitech City can prevent back pain."
- Target Indian health websites like **Practo, Lybrate, HealthKart** for informational guest posts.

## 3. Hospital & Diagnostic Center Partnerships
- Connect with local diagnostic centers in Kukatpally and Miyapur.
- Offer to write "Post-Surgical Rehab Guides" for their patients.
- Request a link from their "Affiliated Services" or "Recommended Care" pages.

## 4. Local Community Portals
- **Miyapur Community Facebook Groups:** Participate in health discussions and share helpful blog links.
- **Kukatpally Housing Board (KPHB) News Portals:** Share local health tips for residents.
- **Hyderabad Apartment Association Portals:** Offer free health workshops in large apartment complexes (e.g., My Home, Aparna) and request a link from their resident portal.

## 5. Educational Backlinks (.edu.in)
- Partner with local physiotherapy colleges in Hyderabad (e.g., Nizam's Institute of Medical Sciences).
- Offer guest lectures or internships and request a link from their "Industry Partners" or "Alumni" section.

## 6. PR & News Mentions
- Share a "New Home Visit Service Launch" press release with local Telugu and English news portals (e.g., Telangana Today, Hans India).
- Focus on "Healthcare Accessibility in Hyderabad" to gain editorial interest.
