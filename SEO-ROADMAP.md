# Flexo Physio: 30-60-90 Day SEO Growth Roadmap

## Phase 1: Foundation (Day 1-30) - Focus: Technical & Local Core
- **GSC & GA4 Setup:** Verify Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4.
- **Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimization:** 
  - Update primary service area to include Kukatpally, Miyapur, Kondapur, Gachibowli, Hitech City.
  - Add "Home Visit" as a primary service feature.
  - Upload 10+ high-quality images of equipment and home sessions.
- **Core Web Vitals:** Ensure LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) is under 2.5s.
- **Initial Content:** Launch the 6 new location landing pages (implemented in Phase 2).

## Phase 2: Authority Building (Day 31-60) - Focus: Content & Reviews
- **Review Campaign:** Implement a system to collect 5-star Google reviews from every home visit patient.
- **Service Expansion:** Create 5 blog posts targeting "Conditions" (e.g., "Post-Stroke Recovery at Home").
- **Local Citations:** Submit business info to local Hyderabad directories (Justdial, Sulekha, Yellow Pages).
- **Internal Linking:** Audit internal links to ensure all service pages are reachable within 2 clicks.

## Phase 3: Scaling & Market Dominance (Day 61-90) - Focus: Backlinks & Video
- **Backlink Outreach:** Connect with local Hyderabad health bloggers and fitness influencers for guest posts.
- **Video SEO:** Create 5 YouTube shorts/Reels demonstrating simple home exercises and embed them in relevant service pages.
- **FAQ Expansion:** Add 5-10 more hyper-local FAQs to location pages (e.g., "Physiotherapy near Miyapur Metro").
- **Performance Review:** Analyze GSC data for new keyword opportunities and refine meta tags.
